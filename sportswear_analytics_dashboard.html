Statistically Supported Relationships — Summary
5 evidence-backed findings with business interpretation
Finding 1
Price does not drive satisfaction
r≈0.08 between price and rating. Customers judge value, not just price. Implication: quality must justify premium.
Finding 2
Quality unlocks organic growth
r=0.42 between rating and reviews. 1pt rating increase drives ~42% more review engagement — a direct marketing multiplier.
Finding 3
Messaging drives perception
r=0.38 sentiment-to-rating. Products with comfort/lightweight emphasis in descriptions consistently rate higher.
Finding 4
Mid-premium is the sweet spot
$50–$90 price range maximises both volume and satisfaction. Premium (>$120) creates value perception challenges without quality signals.
